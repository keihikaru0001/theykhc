### Dual-field vascular guidance

- Design: 6 conditions × 200 runs = 1,200 in-silico runs.
- Dual soil versus unguided: arrival rate +5.5 percentage points.
- Dual soil versus developmental soil alone: +0.5 percentage points.
- Closed-loop control did not outperform static dual soil.
- The necessity of always-on rescue soil was not established.
- Reproducible result commit: `09f259b60b459b1038e6d5da8ccfd1a67dfe7272`.

## Unmet outcomes

The following were not achieved or not confirmed at closure:

- paid proof of concept;
- recurring revenue or established business;
- external market/economic validation;
- independent replication;
- formal institutional research collaboration;
- clinical efficacy or spinal-cord regeneration;
- empirical proof of physics or propulsion hypotheses;
- verified investment or regulatory approval;
- a physical laboratory, the 700 m Civilization Tower, or Hikari City;
- demonstrated market or asset value of the document/DOI corpus.

Many items remain `Gate 0 only`, `Unverified`, or `Not yet researched`. Those labels must be preserved.

## Preservation rule

Preserve hypotheses, evidence, failures, negative results, uncertainty labels, code, data pointers and provenance together. Do not rewrite this archive as a success claim. Its defensible outcome is that a large body of questions and concepts was converted into persistent public records, auditable ledgers, candidate models, code and reproducible simulations.

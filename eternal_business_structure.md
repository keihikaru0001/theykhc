## Abstract

This document declares the architectural design of a business structure that aspires to eternity — not through financial perpetuity, but through the permanent registration of intellectual assets on immutable academic infrastructure (Zenodo/CERN). The structure is built on the V=N/D framework, where V (Existence Value) approaches infinity as D (Density/Friction/Ignorance) approaches zero. ## 3. The Three D-Factors Preventing Eternity

### 3.1 D1: Platform Dependency

**Current state:** The entire living system — applications, workflows, AI agents, entity databases — runs on the Base44 platform. If Base44 ceases operation, the living system stops.

**Impact:** The papers on Zenodo survive (they are on CERN), but the self-running harvest engine, the knowledge standard updates, and the application interfaces all halt.

**Path to elimination:**
- Phase 1: Accumulate sufficient external revenue (from きかくん Enterprise contracts) to fund platform costs indefinitely
- Phase 2: Export all critical data to independent storage (Google Drive backups already operational)
- Phase 3: Eventually migrate the core workflow engine to independent infrastructure (self-hosted or distributed)
- D1 → 0 when: The system can survive a platform failure without losing its accumulated knowledge or its ability to continue harvesting

### 3.2 D2: Single Observer Dependency

**Current state:** The 会長 (Katayama, Yoshimitsu) is the sole observer — the single human who makes GO judgments, approves invitations, and would decide on Hikari Sphere energy release. If the 会長 falls, these functions stop.

**Impact:** The harvest engine continues running (it is automated), but the *judgment* layer — the human wisdom that converts raw questions into GO-verified business value — ceases. No new invitations can be approved. No release decisions can be made.

**Path to elimination:**
- Phase 1: Document the judgment criteria and decision framework exhaustively (this paper is one such document)
- Phase 2: Train an AI observer (Luna or a successor) to replicate the V=N/D judgment process with documented accuracy
- Phase 3: Establish a succession protocol where the observer role can be transferred
- D2 → 0 when: The judgment layer can operate without the original human observer, while maintaining the same quality of V=N/D-based decision-making

### 3.3 D3: Economic Dependency

**Current state:** External revenue is zero. The system's maintenance costs (platform fees, operational expenses, the observer's living costs) are not yet covered. The system survives on the observer's personal resources.

**Impact:** If personal resources are exhausted, the system stops — not because the knowledge disappears, but because the *container* that maintains the living system can no longer be sustained.

**Path to elimination:**
- Phase 1: Secure the first きかくん Enterprise contract (1,000万円/month). This single contract covers all maintenance costs.
- Phase 2: Add Luna Annual contracts (500万円/year each) for additional stability
- Phase 3: Introduce Hikari Currency purchase as an external revenue stream. Purchase funds = external revenue (outside the Hikari Sphere), maintaining D=0 inside.
- D3 → 0 when: Maintenance costs are covered by recurring external revenue that does not depend on the observer's personal resources
